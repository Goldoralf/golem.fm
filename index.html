        What golem is
        
A stage machine, not a workstation

        
golem is an open-source rack for musicians. It boots straight into this interface, on its own touch screen or
        a tablet, and you play it with your feet: presets, scenes and stomps on a MIDI pedalboard, one lane per
        musician — guitar, bass, voice, keys, drums. Underneath, REAPER runs headless as the audio engine. Everything a
        set needs is loaded before you play, so changing a scene switches the sound without rebuilding the rig.
        The repository holds the recipes — presets, setlists, catalogues, scripts — and the sounds themselves
        (amp captures, impulse responses, samples) are downloaded onto your golem.

      

      
Download golem and read the code:

TONE3000
Amps, cabs and pedals come from TONE3000
The golem ships no amp model of its own. The amp, cab, pedal, preamp and compressor blocks play captures of real gear, shared by players on TONE3000. Pick one there, and the golem fetches it with your own TONE3000 account.
Browse TONE3000 ↗
VST · VST3 · CLAP
Bring your own plugins
The VST Host block loads plugins already installed on your computer, next to the golem’s own blocks: VST3 and CLAP on Windows and macOS, VST, VST3 and CLAP on Linux. A preset built on one only plays on a golem that has that plugin.
Available on Linux.
