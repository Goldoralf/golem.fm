Blackwork

Four pedals in black

Met, Emet and Shem are built for the front of a high-gain amp; Golem doubles a voice. Black lacquer, white engraving, and a skull whose eyes light when the pedal is on.

MetA gate that listens to your clean DI, so it closes tight without eating the notes.11 presets
EmetA high-headroom overdrive that pushes a saturated amp into focus.12 presets
ShemA clean, shaped boost that tightens the low end of a high-gain amp.10 presets
