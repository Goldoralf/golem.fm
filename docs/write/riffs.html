Riffs

golem · golem.fm

A riff comes while you play, and is gone the next minute. The golem's Riff catches it with one tap — your instrument, alone, with its dry signal — and sends it to golem.fm, where it can grow into a song.

Write, record or import

On the golem, a riff is recorded: the Riff block is always there, in the ⚙ lane of every preset (Global), next to the recorder. Its settings are the same for every preset; only its footswitch is chosen per preset.

Its drawer says:

  • REC (read-only) — what the recorder will capture: the Riff uses it, so the recorder must record each input on its own track (Multitrack, ideally Multitrack + DI);
  • What Riff records — per person: the lanes on their inputs. A riff is yours: it records your lanes only, never the others';
  • Transcribe on the rack — On: a tab of what you played, read from your dry signal, travels with the riff;
  • Rack footswitch belongs to — whose riff the footswitch records.

The Riff is on in the Home, Jam and Rehearsal modes, off in Pedalboard and Gig.

Capturing a riff on the golem

Tap to start, tap to stop — on the footswitch that carries Riff, on the screen, or from your phone. The recorder's count-in plays first; a second tap during it cancels.

The Live screen follows it: ● Riff <name> while it records, then 🔥 Riff saved — and for 20 seconds, a ✕ Drop button keeps it on the golem and never sends it. After that, 🔥 Riff → golem.fm: only your lanes go, never a mix, never another musician's track. A golem not linked to golem.fm keeps the riff and says so.

When the golem cannot record a riff, it says why instead of making half a take: the mode does not allow it, the footswitch belongs to nobody, you have no lane, a take is already recording, the recorder is set to Mix.

From a riff to a song

A riff lands in golem.fm's composer, titled with its date (Riff 2026-10-22 21:40), where you build on it.

From golem.fm you can also arm a riff to continue: the next tap on the golem records into that riff — the same tempo and bars, with a click — and your phone shows which riff is armed.

The transcription assumes a standard tuning.
