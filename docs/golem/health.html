Health: checks and fixes

golem

Health is the golem's own check-up: what is fine, what is not, and the gesture that fixes it — on the same line. It lives in Settings ▸ System ▸ Health. Open it when something sounds wrong, or when the Live screen shows a warning: a tap on that warning opens Health on the right line.

What Health checks

The banner at the top gives the verdict in one sentence — Playing is safe, then what to look at — and counts the facts that are green, orange and red.

Now lists eleven facts, read every five seconds, touching nothing:

| Fact | What it says | |---|---| | Engine | the audio engine is running and talks to the golem | | Sound card | the card is there and its audio really moves | | Dropouts | clicks in the sound in the last ten minutes — a few is orange, many is red | | Pedalboard & MIDI | the pedalboard and your MIDI devices answer | | Playing | the preset that plays has all its files loaded | | Setlist on the foot | the blocks of your setlist that will not play on this golem, and why | | Plugins | what the engine found installed | | Disk | the free space — orange under 20 GB, red under 5 GB | | golem.fm | paired, and reachable | | Scripts | no error in the golem's own scripts since it started | | Since last boot | what changed on the machine since it started |

Grey means not measured yet, or does not apply: it never alarms. On a new golem, Setlist on the foot is orange — the factory presets need their captures from TONE3000 — and its Patches → button goes where they are downloaded.

Fixing from Health

When the golem knows the answer to an orange or red line, the line carries a Fix button: raise the audio buffer after dropouts, reopen the sound card, reopen the MIDI devices after a cable was replugged, pose the preset again when files did not load, restart the engine.

Nothing runs on the first tap. The sheet first says what will change (before → after), what it costs, how the golem will check that it worked, and how to undo it. Confirm, and the golem runs it, then verifies: the line turns green, or the sheet says it did not work.

Health never fixes anything while a take records, a song plays or the screen is locked: the line says so and waits. The bottom of the column says whether the golem is free for a check.

Check now runs a check on demand, each one with what it costs:

  • Why no sound? — read-only: you play, the golem follows the signal along the chain and names the first place where it goes silent;
  • Inputs, output, pedalboard — the first-install check again (about two minutes): play, listen to the click, press a switch.

Ask golem

When your golem is paired with golem.fm, the banner has an ✦ Ask golem field: say what happens in your own words — it crackles, no sound from the keys. The question goes to golem.fm with the state of the golem; the answer comes back with the help pages that apply and, at most, one proposed fix — which opens the same sheet as the Fix button, and is verified the same way. Your golem asks for your consent once, before the first question.

Incidents and reports

Incidents keeps the last 30 days. A line that stays red opens an incident; when it turns green again, the incident closes with how it ended — by a fix, by a restart, or by itself. When it came back by itself, the golem may ask What fixed it? — a cable, the gain, the power — so the next person with the same problem gets a better answer.

Incidents travel with the golem's diagnostic reports to golem.fm: what happened and how it ended, never your song titles or your names.
