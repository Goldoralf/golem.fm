Storage and back-ups

golem

Your presets, songs, captures and recordings live on the golem's own disk. Settings ▸ Content ▸ Storage shows what takes the space, where your song folders are, and when each part of your music was last backed up.

Storage

This golem's disk shows the whole drive: your music, the installed sounds (pianos, drum kits), the caches, the system — and what is free.

Your music on this golem lists your content by kind — amps, recordings, songs, standards, presets and patches, kits and grooves… — with its size and the age of its last backup: a red dot means never or more than 30 days, amber more than 7.

Song folders are folders of .golem songs that the golem reads where they are — on this disk, an external drive, a network share — and never writes into. Add them with + Add folders. A song goes onto the golem itself when you use it there — add it to a setlist, change its sheet, or tap Use on this golem: what goes on stage is on the golem. An unplugged drive erases nothing: its songs come back with it.

The drop folder (~/golem-inbox by default) works the other way: a .golem dropped into it is copied into the golem, then moved aside — never deleted.

Caches rebuild on their own — waveform previews, songs unpacked to play, golem.fm stems. Clear frees their space; nothing of yours is in them.

Back-ups, local and vault

⇅ Back up… opens Backups, in three tabs.

  • Export — tick categories (presets and scenes, patches, setlists, settings, captures, kits, songs, recordings…) and ⬇ Download backup makes one file. Each line says its size and its last backup. Your keys, your golem.fm pairing, your paired devices and your Wi-Fi password are never in a backup.
  • Import — drop a backup, or pick one already on the golem or on golem.fm. Nothing is written before you see the plan: what is new, what is different here, what is identical. For what differs: Keep mine, Use the backup or Keep both. The golem keeps a safety copy before it writes, and never replaces a capture or a recording that is already there.
  • Vault — your backups on the golem and on golem.fm, by date; ‹ newer · older › pages through them. Tick Also keep a copy on golem.fm when you export, or let every new backup go there automatically.

Songs stay on the golem: a backup that contains your songs is never sent to golem.fm, which never keeps a cover's audio. ⬇ Back up everything now saves everything except the songs — export the songs on their own.

Some sounds are not backed up because the installer puts them back: the large drum kits, the sampled pianos, the golem.fm stems.

Before a reinstall

  1. Export everything, then the songs, and keep the two files off the golem — a USB key, another computer, or golem.fm's vault for the first one.
  2. Write down what is not in a backup: your TONE3000 key, your Wi-Fi password. You will pair golem.fm and TONE3000 again; your golem number stays the same.
  3. After the reinstall, Import both files from Settings ▸ Content ▸ Storage ▸ ⇅ Back up….

The uninstaller makes this backup for you on its first screen (to a folder of your choice, or a USB drive) and also keeps the golem's identity and your REAPER licence.
