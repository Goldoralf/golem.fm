Lanes: one per musician

golem

A golem plays a whole band, not one guitar. Each musician has a lane: their own input, their own chain of blocks, their own sound in every scene. This page opens Metal for all, the factory preset with the most lanes (line 07 of the Factory Golem setlist, Iron Tide).

What a lane is

A lane is one instrument's path through the golem: an input, a chain of blocks, an output.

  • One tab per lane, at the top of the Designer. Metal for all has six: Guitar 1, Bass 1, Guitar 2, Voice 1, Drums and Keys. Tap a tab to see that lane's chain.

  • The input is on the left of the chain, the output on the right. Guitar 1 plays from IN 1 and goes out on outputs 1·2.

  • The chain is everything in between. Guitar 1 here: a Whammy, a gate and an EQ, then two amps on two branches, then the effects that follow.

All the lanes play at the same time. A scene changes all of them together — see Presets and scenes.

Guitar, bass, voice, keys, drums

Every lane has a role: guitar, bass, voice, keys, pads, drums, or line. The role decides two things: which input the lane looks for, and which blocks make sense on it — a voice lane is never offered an amp or a cabinet.

  • Audio lanes — guitar, bass, voice, line — play from a jack of your sound card.
  • MIDI lanes — keys, pads, drums — play from a USB port of the rack, where a keyboard, a pad controller or an electronic drum kit is plugged. The golem makes their sound: a piano, a synth, a drum kit.

The tab tells you where each lane plays from: IN 1 for a jack, USB 1 for a USB port.

A drum lane turns the hits of an electronic kit into the sound of the rack's kits. See Keys, drums and samples.

One input per musician

A preset never names a jack. It says "Guitar 1", "Voice 1" — and your sound card says which jack is which. That is why a preset made on another golem plays on yours.

  • The card's inputs have roles. On the reference rack, a Volt 276 just plugged, INPUT 1 is the guitar and INPUT 2 has no role yet. You give each jack its instrument in Settings ▸ Gear ▸ Soundcard.

  • A lane finds its input by role: the first guitar lane takes the first guitar input, the second takes the second. Two adjacent jacks can be linked into one stereo instrument.
  • A lane whose instrument is not on this card waits. Its tab says IN —. The golem never plugs it into another jack by guesswork: a voice lane on a guitar jack would be worse than silence. Give the input a role on the Soundcard page and the lane mounts.
