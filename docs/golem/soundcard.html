Sound card and inputs

golem

Your sound card is where the instruments come in and the sound goes out. Settings ▸ Gear ▸ Soundcard draws its back panel and says, for each jack, which instrument is plugged in. That is what lets your presets work on any card: a preset never names a jack, it asks for Guitar 1 or Voice 1.

Inputs and roles

The page has two rows. CARD is the card you plugged in: its inputs, its outputs, its headphone jack. Above the row, its name and its buffer (Volt 276 · 192 smp · 8 ms).

Tap an input to say what is plugged into it: a guitar, a bass, a voice, keys, drums, a line. The golem names it for you — Guitar 1, Voice 1, Guitar 2 — and you can say who plays it. On the reference rack, input 1 is the guitar and input 2 is free.

  • A preset asks for an instrument, never a jack. Its Guitar 1 lane plays whatever input is Guitar 1 on this card. Take the same preset to a 16-input interface: it finds Guitar 1 there too.
  • An instrument this card does not have is said, not guessed. A lane that needs Guitar 2 on a card with one guitar waits, greyed, with the reason — it never lands on whatever happens to be input 2 (on a Volt, the microphone).
  • The setup belongs to the card. Plug another card, and the golem loads that card's setup; plug the first one back, it is still there.

The outputs are named the same way: here OUT L and OUT R are the Monitors, and the headphone jack mirrors them (see The mixer).

Stereo pairs

A drum module or a keyboard often comes out on two jacks, left and right: one instrument. The ⛓ link between two neighbouring inputs joins them into one stereo instrument; tap it again to split them. Outputs pair the same way: OUT L and OUT R form one pair here.

USB and MIDI

The second row, RACK, belongs to the golem itself, whatever the card: USB 1 to 4 and the MIDI jacks. It never moves when you change cards — a keyboard plugged into the golem stays plugged in.

  • A USB port carries a controller: keys, pads, both, or an electronic drum kit. Bind it here so lanes can play it (Keys + Pads 1). An unbound port is greyed, with what is plugged into it: on the reference rack, USB 1 · Alesis Nitro · unbound.
  • When you plug something new, the banner at the top says what was detected and what to do with it.
  • MIDI IN and MIDI OUT are for the golem's own gear: the pedalboard (here the FCB1010) and a MIDI-controlled pedal (here a Whammy DT). They are not instruments.

The details of each controller are on the next tab — see Controllers: keyboards, pads, expression.

Card setups

  • A new card is detected when you plug it: the golem offers to switch to it. Switching restarts the audio engine (about ten seconds); the old card's setup is kept for when it comes back.
  • The card is unplugged? Every page says so, with a button per card that is plugged in. The golem never switches by itself: a gig output silently moving to another card is worse than a visible silence.
  • A card you do not have yet can be prepared: declare it from Settings ▸ Mode (a mode can wait for a given card), name its inputs at home, and it is ready the day you plug it in.
  • The same card on two golems — your home rig and a second rack, or Windows and Linux on one computer — can be cabled differently. When both are linked to your golem.fm account, a ⇄ chip appears on this page: compare the two setups, use the other one, offer yours, or keep them separate. A golem is never changed from afar: the setup is applied here, in front of the card.
