Controllers: keyboards, pads, expression

golem

A controller is what you play with your hands or feet that is not an audio instrument: a USB keyboard, a pad controller, the expression pedals of your pedalboard. The golem probes what it can and learns the rest from you — once, on the golem, without a manual.

Keyboards and pads

Settings ▸ Gear ▸ Controllers lists the USB keyboards and pad controllers the golem sees. A plugged controller says ● connected, the USB port it is on, and whether it is known yet.

To play it, bind it on the Soundcard tab: tap its USB port and say what it is — keys, pads, or both. It becomes an instrument, Keys + Pads 1, that a lane can play:

A controller stays known once it has been connected: unplug it, it is simply not active, and its settings wait for it.

Calibration

Bound, a controller plays. Calibrated, every key, pad and knob of it is known. Calibration is a short series of questions, each one skippable:

  1. Identity — read by itself: the model, its USB name, its ports.
  2. Keyboard — press the lowest key, the highest, then middle C. The golem records the range and the channel the keys play on.
  3. Pads — hit each pad when the screen asks: the golem builds the pad map.
  4. Knobs — turn each knob in turn.
  5. Extras — sustain pedal, pitch and mod wheels. The arpeggiator is checked, not guessed: turn it on, hold a note, and the golem verifies the notes arrive in time with its clock.

Pads on their own channel. Many controllers send pads and keys on the same channel, with the same notes: no software can tell a pad from a key. The real fix is inside the controller, and the golem can make it for known models (the MPK Mini Mk II is one): Give the pads their own channel keeps a copy of the controller's setting, writes the pads on channel 10 with standard drum notes, reads it back, then asks you to hit pad 1 again. For another model, set it in the maker's editor and calibrate again.

Expression pedals

The two expression pedals of the pedalboard each have a role:

  • Volume — a volume block in your chain, like a volume pedal on a pedalboard;
  • Effect — the knob of a block you choose: the sweep of a wah, the pitch of a whammy, a delay's mix.

The roles are switched in Settings ▸ Gear ▸ Pedalboard, without reflashing anything. Which block the Effect pedal drives is chosen in the block itself: its drawer's MIDI assign tab (see Blocks). On the Live screen, the two bars at the bottom show both pedals and what they drive — see The pedalboard.
