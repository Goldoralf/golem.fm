Factory content

golem

Every golem ships with content to play on the first day: presets, patches, a setlist, a song, grooves and standards. It is marked ◆ factory everywhere you meet it. You can hide it, change it, delete it — and get it back.

What every golem ships with

Settings ▸ Content ▸ Factory counts it, kind by kind, and says where each kind lives:

| Kind | How many | Where | |---|---|---| | Patches | 38 | Songs ▸ Patches — sounds for guitar, bass, voice, keys and drums | | Presets | 9 | Songs ▸ Presets — from Chill Jazz to Metal for all | | Banks & Setlists | 6 | Songs ▸ Banks & Setlists — the Factory Golem setlist and one bank per instrument | | Pad kits | 3 | Library ▸ Drums ▸ Kits | | Grooves | 26 | Library ▸ Drums ▸ Grooves — for the drum machine | | Standards | 3 | Library ▸ Tracks ▸ Standards — chord charts the band plays along |

And one song, Golem Rising (Songs ▸ Songs), the last line of the Factory Golem setlist, with its arrangement.

The Factory Golem setlist is the one the factory modes put on the foot (Settings ▸ Mode ▸ Foot list): nine songs, one per factory preset, ready to play.

Hiding and restoring

Each kind has a Shown / Hidden switch. Hidden factory content disappears from its pages — your own content stays alone on screen — but nothing is deleted: switch it back, it is there.

Deleting is different. A factory item you delete stays deleted: a golem never puts back what you removed. Restore factory content lists what you deleted, with a Restore button each (and Restore all).

When golem updates its factory content, a new version replaces the old one by itself — as long as you did not change it. A factory item you changed on this golem stays yours: updates skip it, and the page lists it under Changed on this golem. Review shows them; Reset puts the factory version back (tap twice: your changes are lost).

Downloading the captures

The factory presets and patches use amps, pedals and cabinets from TONE3000. golem does not ship them: they come from your TONE3000 account. Until they are here, the blocks that need them are off and the rest of the chain plays — see Captures.

Factory sounds counts them — 0 of 35 captures on this rack on a new golem — and ⬇ Download the factory captures fetches them all in one go. It needs your account connected first (Settings ▸ Connect ▸ TONE3000).
