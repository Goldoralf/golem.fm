TONE3000 does not connect

golem · golem.fm

When the golem cannot download from TONE3000, it says why — on the missing block, in the Factory page, and in Settings ▸ Connect ▸ TONE3000.

Reconnecting

| The golem says | What to do | |---|---| | TONE3000 not signed in | the golem never signed in: tap 📱 Connect and sign in on your phone | | TONE3000 session expired | sign in again: ↻ Sign in again | | sign-in refused on TONE3000 | the sign-in was cancelled on the phone: tap Connect again | | the sign-in expired | a QR is valid ten minutes: tap Connect again | | golem.fm is not reachable · golem.fm cannot relay TONE3000 sign-ins yet | the golem needs golem.fm to sign in: check its internet, or use your own key under Advanced | | TONE3000 key missing | only for a golem without golem.fm: paste your own key (t3k_pub_…, from tone3000.com ▸ Settings ▸ API Keys), save with 💾, then connect |

With your own key, the golem changed network? The return address you declared on tone3000.com is the golem's address on its network: a new router or the golem's own Wi-Fi changes it. Declare the new one, then sign in again. Through golem.fm, nothing to declare: the network does not matter.

Signing in again never loses what you downloaded: the captures stay on the golem.

One connection per machine

Each golem keeps its own TONE3000 session. TONE3000 renews it at each use and accepts one user per session: two programs sharing one sign each other out, and TONE3000 then closes the session for both.

  • Do not copy a golem's settings to another machine to "share" the connection: sign in on each golem.
  • A second golem, a second computer: same account, its own sign-in.
  • golem.fm and your golem are separate too: being signed in on golem.fm does not sign your golem in.
