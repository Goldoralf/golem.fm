Doc / Troubleshooting

No sound

golem

No sound comes out. Do not restart anything yet: the golem can tell where the sound stops, and a restart erases what it would have found. Go from the instrument to the speakers.

Inputs and levels

Is the sound coming in? On the golem's screen:

  • ⚠ IN 1 CLIP on the Live screen — the input is too hot: turn the gain of the input on the sound card down, not the preset.
  • No signal at all — the cable, the instrument's volume, the input gain of the card. Settings ▸ System ▸ Health ▸ Check now ▸ Inputs, output, pedalboard replays the first-install check: plug into any input and play, and the golem says which input it hears, and whether the gain is low, good or hot (see The first-install check).

Headphones fine, speakers silent? The sound leaves the golem: look after it — the MONITOR knob of the sound card, the speakers' power and volume, their cables.

⚠ NO CARD — the sound card is not there: every page says … is not plugged in. Plug it back in; the golem reopens it by itself. It never moves your sound to another card on its own.

The lane and the preset

The sound comes in, but the preset does not play it:

  • The lane waits for an input this card does not have. A preset asking for Guitar 2 on a one-guitar card does not play that lane: the Designer greys its tab and says why. Choose another input, or patch it on Settings ▸ Gear ▸ Soundcard (see Sound card and inputs).
  • ⚠ FX FILE on the Live screen — a capture or an IR did not load: the golem reloads it. A capture that is not on this golem leaves its block off and the rest plays (see Captures).
  • ⚠ RIG — the last preset could not be built: open it in the Designer, which says what is wrong.
  • ⚠ NO AUDIO — the audio engine stopped moving: the golem tries to reopen the card once, by itself.
  • The mode may leave an instrument out (Settings ▸ Mode ▸ Inputs), and the mixer may have a strip down or muted (Live ▸ Mixer).

Each of these warnings is a tap away from its line in Health — except in a locked mode, where they only inform.

Health ▸ silence

Settings ▸ System ▸ Health ▸ Check now ▸ Why no sound? — read-only: play, and for a few seconds the golem follows your signal along the chain. It names the first place where the sound stops:

| The golem says | Look at | |---|---| | No signal reaches the input tracks. | the cable, the card's input and its gain | | The signal enters, then stops inside the chain. | the block it names — often a capture or an IR not loaded | | The signal leaves the golem — the silence is after it. | the card's MONITOR knob, the speakers, their cables | | The sound card is lost — not the rig. | the card's USB cable, then Health's Fix to reopen it |

It is refused while a take records or a song plays (Not now). When the line in Now is red, its Fix button does the repair — and checks it worked (see Health).
