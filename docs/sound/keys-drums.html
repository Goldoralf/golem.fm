Keys, drums and samples

golem · golem.fm

Besides the captures of your amps and pedals, the golem plays instruments of its own: pianos, an organ, synths, drum kits — and samples you fire from the pedalboard.

Instruments of the rack

Library ▸ Keys holds what a Keys lane can play:

  • Instruments — the rack's own: - Piano — the Salamander Grand, a sampled concert grand. It is an optional pack of the installer (714 MB); without it, the card says so; - E-Piano — an FM electric piano (a DX7), with a bank of 32 sounds; bank and sound are chosen per scene; - Organ — a tonewheel organ: drawbars, Leslie and percussion are knobs of the block, per scene. There is no file: the sound is the engine.
  • Synths — Vital presets, grouped by pack. + Add imports .vital files and banks. To use one, assign it to a scene in the Designer, on a Keys lane.

Drum kits and grooves

Library ▸ Drums has three tabs:

  • Drums — the rack's drum kits, played by a drum lane or the Drum Machine: Black Pearl and Red Zeppelin (rock), Blonde Bop (jazz, brushes), and Aasimonster, a large multi-mic metal kit that comes with an optional pack of the installer. + Add imports other kits;
  • Kits — pad kits for a pad controller: House, Lo-fi and Trap, sixteen pads each;
  • Grooves — the patterns of the Drum Machine:

26 ship with golem, two bars each: funk, rock, shuffle, half-time, four on the floor, boom bap, one drop, bossa, swing, a 6/8 ballad, house, lo-fi, trap — and a rock and a metal set with an intro, verse, chorus, solo, fill and ending, so that a song's arrangement can pick a groove per part. + Add imports .mid files.

Samples

Library ▸ Samples has two tabs, and the tab you drop a file into decides what it is:

  • Short — one-shots: kicks, snares, claps, hi-hats, toms, cymbals, percussion. On a new golem, the sounds of the three pad kits. Sample kits are ordered sets of sounds that a footswitch steps through;
  • Songs — long files: a bounce, an intro, a pad. The Sample block plays them, and a song's arrangement can place them on its bars.

A sample used by a preset says so, and cannot be deleted while it is.

Settings ▸ Content ▸ Factory can hide the factory grooves, pad kits and standards — nothing is deleted.
