The looper

golem

The looper records what you play and plays it back under you: a chord pad, then a guitar riff, then a voice on top. On the golem it is a preset of its own — Loop Station — where four switches of your pedalboard become four loops.

The Loop Station preset

Loop Station is line 08 of the Factory Golem setlist. Its bottom row is no longer scenes: it is the looper.

  • #1 to #4 are the loops. Each one records one instrument: on Loop Station, #1 records the keys and pads, #2 the guitar, #3 and #4 the voice. A loop records the sound as you hear it — scene and effects included — and keeps playing when you change scene.
  • #5 is ▶ ALL: play or stop every loop together. It also shows the length of the loop that set the tempo.
  • #6 to #8 stay ordinary stomps — here a chorus, a tempo delay and a shimmer.
  • #9 is REC, the golem's recorder, and #10 clears everything — hold it about a second; a short tap does nothing.
  • EXP 1 is the level of all the loops, EXP 2 the volume.

Which instrument each loop records, and on which switch, is set in the looper's drawer (the ⚙ lane of the preset): up to four loops, and two loops can record the same instrument — a rhythm part and a lead.

Recording, overdub, undo on the foot

On a loop switch:

| The loop is… | Gesture | What happens | |---|---|---| | empty | press | it records, from the moment you press | | recording | press | it closes and plays | | playing | tap | it stops | | stopped | tap | it plays again, in time with the others | | playing | double tap | overdub: you record a new layer on it; double tap again to stop layering | | playing or stopped | hold 0.6 s | this loop is cleared |

  • The first loop sets the length for the others. The looper's Loop length decides how: free — the loop is exactly as long as you played it (Loop Station is set this way); auto — when a click, the drum machine or a backing plays, the golem closes that first loop on a whole bar, so you can press a little late, and with nothing playing it counts you in: one bar of click, and the loop starts on the 1.
  • Clearing is the undo: hold a loop to erase it and record it again; hold #10 to start over with all four.
  • Each loop has its level in the Live mixer, one strip per loop, so a loud pad never buries the riff.

The loops play the audio they recorded: changing scene afterwards changes what you play over them, never what they hold.
