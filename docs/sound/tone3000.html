Connect TONE3000

golem · golem.fm

TONE3000 is where the captures of amps, pedals and cabinets come from — the factory presets name theirs, and you choose yours. The golem downloads them with your TONE3000 account.

Why each account connects its own

golem does not ship captures: they belong to the people who made them, on TONE3000. Each golem therefore signs in to TONE3000 on its own, with your account, and keeps that session on the machine. Even when golem.fm asks your golem to install a tone, it is the golem that downloads it, with its own session.

One golem, one session. TONE3000 renews a session each time it is used, and refuses two programs sharing the same one: they would sign each other out. A second golem signs in on its own. Your golem and golem.fm each keep their own session with your account, side by side: neither signs the other out.

On golem.fm

golem.fm has its own TONE3000 session, for what you do on the site: Profile ▸ API ▸ Connect TONE3000, sign in on TONE3000, and you come back to the page you left with TONE3000 connected.

It is what lets you search and listen to captures in Library ▸ Tones ▸ + Import a sound, what the patch builder uses to pick the captures of a chain, and what adds a recipe's captures from the Store. A capture imported on golem.fm joins your library; your golem downloads it on its side, with its own session.

Connecting golem.fm does not connect your golem, and the other way round: each one signs in once.

On the golem

Settings ▸ Connect ▸ TONE3000:

  1. Tap 📱 Connect. The golem shows a QR code — on a computer, a link to open instead.
  2. Scan it with your phone and sign in to TONE3000. The phone says you can go back to your golem.
  3. The panel turns green by itself: Connected to TONE3000.

This needs your golem to be paired with golem.fm (see Link the golem to golem.fm): golem.fm passes the sign-in on to your golem, which keeps the session to itself. Nothing to paste, no address to declare.

A golem without golem.fm connects with your own TONE3000 key — the page shows it when the golem is not paired, and under Advanced otherwise: create a key on tone3000.com ▸ Settings ▸ API Keys, paste it and save with 💾, tap Connect, and declare once on tone3000.com the return address the golem shows.

From then on, ⬇ Download on a missing capture, ⬇ Download all in the Designer and ⬇ Download the factory captures in Settings ▸ Content ▸ Factory all use this session — see Captures.
