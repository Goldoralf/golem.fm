Captures (NAM) and impulse responses

golem · golem.fm

The amps, drive pedals and cabinets of your rig are captures: recordings of real gear, measured and turned into a model the golem plays. Amps and pedals are NAM captures; cabinets are impulse responses (IRs). The golem does not ship them: they come from TONE3000, through your own account.

Amps, pedals, cabinets

In a preset, a capture sits in a block like any other: the amp block of Ambient Metal plays a Peavey 6505+ capture. The preset names the capture; the file lives in your Tones library.

  • An amp capture is a head alone (add a cab block after it) or a whole rig, cabinet included (+Cab).
  • A pedal capture is a drive, a fuzz, a boost — the pedal as it was set when it was captured.
  • A cabinet is an IR: the speaker and the microphone in front of it.
  • Outboard gear — a preamp, a compressor — is captured the same way.

golem's own effects (delays, reverbs, modulation, EQ…) are not captures: they are part of the golem and never need a download.

Your Tones library

Library ▸ Tones holds your captures, in four tabs: Amps, Pedals, Cabs, Outboard. The role at the top (Guitar, Bass, Voice) shows what that instrument can load.

The Amps tab is grouped by brand. Each card is an amp model — a 5150, a Powerball, a Savage — with the captures of it on this golem under its name. On a new golem every card says no capture yet.

The filters narrow the list: Heads / +Cab, the gain (Clean, Crunch, High-gain) and the style.

Tap a model: it lists your captures of it, and ⬇ Browse TONE3000 captures finds more. To use one, add the amp in the Designer (the + of the lane): the picker opens filtered on that model.

Missing captures

A preset made elsewhere — a factory preset, a song from your band — names captures this golem may not have. The Designer says it at the top: ⚠ 9 blocks missing on this rack, and ⬇ Download all fetches them from TONE3000 in one go.

A missing block is off, the rest of the chain plays. You never lose the sound of a whole preset because of one capture. Its tile is marked; tap it:

  • ⬇ Download — from TONE3000: the block plays as soon as the file lands.
  • ↺ Pick another capture… — from this golem or from TONE3000.
  • Remove block — this preset no longer uses it.
  • Keep it off — leave it as it is: it plays on a golem that has the capture.

Downloading needs your TONE3000 account connected on this golem (Settings ▸ Connect ▸ TONE3000). The captures of every factory preset are fetched in one gesture from Settings ▸ Content ▸ Factory — see Factory content.
