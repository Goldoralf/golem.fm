Devices and access

golem · golem.fm

Every phone, tablet or computer that opens your golem is paired: it holds its own key, given by the golem. Settings ▸ Connect ▸ Devices shows who has one, adds new ones, and takes them back.

Pairing a device

When your golem is linked to golem.fm, the page has one card per member of your band: their instruments, their inputs, their devices. ＋ Add a device for … shows a QR: the person scans it with the phone's camera, and the Companion opens on their lines — one device, valid 15 minutes. ＋ Invite to … shows golem.fm's QR to join the band: the golem never creates members itself.

Without golem.fm, there is no band: every device is a guest.

  • Guests — no golem.fm account, and access ends on its own: ＋ Guest for a day, a week or a month, a name, a duration. The guest's row counts the days left; Extend gives more. A guest's name never leaves the golem.
  • Rack screens — a tablet used as the golem's own screen: ▣ Pairing code for a screen gives a six-digit code to type on the tablet, at golem.local/app/pair.html — one use, ten minutes. A rack screen has all rights.

On a device's row: 🎼 makes a phone the conductor (it loads, starts and stops the songs), ★ makes a device an admin (network, devices, updates), 🗑 revokes it.

The golem's own touch screen is always admin, and is never listed: it cannot be revoked.

Revoking access

  • 🗑 on a row, then sure?, takes the key back from that device. The device then says This device is no longer paired with the rack and offers to pair again.
  • ↻ Revoke all remote devices, tapped twice, takes back every key and every pending invitation; the golem's own screen keeps working.
  • A guest's access expires by itself at the end of its duration.

Your golem itself is revoked from golem.fm, not from the golem (see Link the golem to golem.fm).
