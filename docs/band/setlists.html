Setlists

golem · golem.fm

A setlist is one evening: the songs in the order you play them. On the golem it is also what your feet play — bank 01 is the first song, bank 02 the second, and the up and down switches walk through it.

Building a setlist

Songs ▸ Banks & Setlists shows two kinds of lists:

  • a setlist (▤ SETLIST) holds songs — each line plays a song, with its preset, its tempo, its arrangement;
  • a bank (⛓ BANK) holds sounds — each line plays a patch. The factory banks give each instrument its sounds, one per bank: Factory Guitar, Factory Bass, Factory Keys…

Every golem ships with the Factory Golem setlist: nine songs, one per factory preset. Open it.

The songs are on the left in bank order: the number is the bank. A song line says its preset, its tempo (♩ 120), and small icons for what it carries (an arrangement, stems).

+ Add a song picks a song from your repertoire (Songs ▸ Songs). A setlist never creates a song: a song exists once, and every setlist that plays it follows it.

Banks on the foot

⇅ Banks opens the grid. The grid is the pedalboard: bank 01 is the first one you reach.

  • 19 banks, no more. A setlist is one gig and must fit the banks your feet reach directly. The golem refuses a twentieth song rather than drop one silently.
  • A free bank is real. Leave a hole on purpose — for an encore you may play. Pressing an empty bank is safe: it never changes what plays.
  • Tap a free bank to place a preset there. Tap a filled one, then another bank, to move it (two filled banks swap).

Only one list is on the foot at a time. On a list's card, ▶ On the foot puts it there (tap twice: it changes what your feet do). On the Live screen, tap the list's name in the top bar:

Tap a list, then tap it again to confirm: it goes on the foot and its first song loads. Each mode can also put its own list on the foot when you enter it (Settings ▸ Mode ▸ Foot list).

Tonight-only changes

Tap a song line: the right side shows what it plays — its preset, tempo, tuning and capo, the players of its preset (Click, Backing, Drums…) and its arrangement.

On the golem, a song line is read here and changed in the song. ✎ Edit the song › opens it: change the tempo there, and every setlist that plays Golem Rising follows.

An exception for one evening only — tonight in Drop D, tonight at 110 — lives on the line, not in the song: the other setlists keep the song as it is. The golem plays the exceptions a setlist brings from golem.fm, where they are made; on the golem itself, every change goes into the song.

A line can also carry no song at all: a guest, an a cappella moment, a song not written yet. Such a free line is edited right here (preset, tempo, tuning), and ◆ Make it a song turns it into a song of your repertoire.

Playing it on the golem

With the setlist on the foot, the bank switches change song: the golem loads the song's preset with the song's tempo, stems and arrangement. On the Live screen, the top bar says Factory Golem 9/9 and the song bar shows SETLIST #09 · 9/9 above the song's title.

  • ◀ ▶ on the Live screen do the same as the foot: previous and next song.
  • A preset loaded from somewhere else (the Designer, the preset browser) plays on its own: its sounds, not its song — no parts, no stems, no click. The song bar says no song · sounds only — load it; tap it, or press the bank switch, to load the song.
  • During a Gig, a setlist that arrives from golem.fm and touches the list on the foot waits until you leave the mode: your set never changes under your feet.

A setlist holds references: if you change a preset, the setlist plays the new sound. To freeze the sounds of a set as they are tonight — before you rework your presets — take a snapshot (📷); it is the only way back to tonight's sounds.
