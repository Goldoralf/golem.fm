Click and tempo

golem

The click is the golem's metronome. The tempo is set once per song and everything follows it: the click, the delays synced to the beat, the drum machine, the arrangement's bars, the looper's first loop.

The Click

The Click lives in the ⚙ lane of every preset, under Global: every preset means it is one click for the whole golem — set it once, every song uses it. Only its footswitch is chosen per preset.

Its drawer sets:

  • Level — from −60 to +6 dB;
  • Beats / bar — 2 to 7. A song whose meter changes says it in its arrangement: each part has its time signature, and the click follows it;
  • the output — the card output that carries the click role, the monitors by default. Send it to your in-ears and never to the room.

The click is never recorded in the golem's takes: it is for you, not for the song.

When does it sound? The mode decides (Settings ▸ Mode ▸ Click):

  • Off — never, whatever you press;
  • Per preset — the Click block of the loaded preset decides: active, it plays;
  • Manual — a plain on/off, the same for every song.

A song with an arrangement can also start the click with the song and stop it at the end: tick Click in its When the song starts row (see The arrangement). Its count-in always sounds, even when the click stays silent for the song.

Tempo per song

A tempo is set at three levels; the most precise wins:

  1. the preset — the BPM at the top of the Designer, and in ⚙ Global. It is the tempo of the rig when you load it on its own;
  2. the song — the Tempo of its song page (Songs ▸ Songs). Empty means the preset's. Golem Rising plays Ambient Metal at 120;
  3. the setlist line — a line without a song carries its own tempo; an exception for one evening arrives with the setlist from golem.fm.

When a song loads from the setlist, the golem sets its tempo before anything plays: the click, the delays set to a note value, the drum machine and the bars of the arrangement all take it. The Live screen and the Designer show the tempo that is playing.

The golem has no tap tempo: a song's tempo is written in the song, not tapped on stage. To change it, open the song (Songs ▸ Songs) or, for a preset on its own, its BPM in the Designer.
