The first-install check

golem

The first time your golem starts, it checks itself — and then asks you three things only you can do: play, listen, press a switch. Two minutes, once, and you know the golem works on your gear.

The first-install check

The check opens by itself after the first boot, in place of the Live screen. The top lines are what the golem checked alone: the engine, the sound card, the factory content, the first preset and its files. Then come the three gestures:

  1. Plug and play — your instrument in, sound measured;
  2. Listen — the click plays on the main outputs;
  3. Press a switch — the pedalboard answers.

Skip for now always leaves. You can run the check again any time from Settings ▸ System ▸ Health ▸ Check now ▸ Inputs, output, pedalboard.

Guitar in, sound out

Plug into any input and play. The golem watches every input of the card and keeps the one where it hears you — We hear IN 2. What is plugged in? The instrument the card already expects is pre-selected; confirm it, or tap another: guitar, bass, voice, keys, line.

While you play, it judges the input gain of your card, live, as you turn the knob:

  • low — under −24 dBFS: turn the gain up on the card;
  • good;
  • hot — over −3 dBFS, or the input clipped: turn it down.

Then Listen: the golem starts the click on the main outputs and asks whether you hear it. Then Press a switch: any switch of the pedalboard, within thirty seconds.

When something is missing

The last screen gives the verdict. Ready: sound in, sound out, the pedalboard answers — play. Otherwise it says what is missing, in the order it matters:

  • No signal came in — check the cable and the input gain, then try again: nothing else can be judged without sound in;
  • One adjustment: the input gain — everything works, the level is not right yet;
  • Sound comes in but the click was not heard — check that the monitors are on and plugged into the main output;
  • Almost ready: one thing left — the pedalboard did not answer. You can play from the screen; Try the pedalboard again replays only that gesture.

Nothing to plug in yet? Nothing to plug in — skip leaves that gesture. The golem remembers what it saw on day one: if something stops working later, Health and golem.fm know it used to work.
