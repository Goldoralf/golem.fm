Use your phone on stage

golem · golem.fm

Each musician can have the golem on their phone: the Companion. It shows your lines in the song — your scene, your pedals, what comes next — and lets you mark a moment of the take. One golem, everyone's phone.

Joining the rack without typing an IP

Two ways in, no address to type:

  • With internet — open golem.fm/connect on your phone, signed in to golem.fm: your golem is there, tap Open (see Link the golem to golem.fm).
  • Without internet — on the golem, Settings ▸ Connect ▸ Devices, your card, ＋ Add a device for …: the golem shows a QR. Scan it with the phone's camera; the Companion opens, already yours. On stage with the golem's own Wi-Fi, the Wi-Fi tab shows two QR codes: the first joins the network, the second opens golem.

The golem answers at http://golem.local on its network. Once paired, the phone stays paired — at home and on stage, without internet. Add it to your home screen: it opens as the golem Companion.

Your lane on the phone

The Companion shows the lines of its person: the lanes whose input belongs to you on the golem (Settings ▸ Mode ▸ Inputs, or Settings ▸ Gear ▸ Soundcard). Its tabs:

  • Now — the song loaded, what to prepare (Tune to Standard, how it starts), your lines in this song and their scene;
  • Song — the song's parts and where the band is;
  • Mine — your pedals: tap to switch a stomp, your scene's level, a tuner;
  • Lyrics — for a singer, when the song has them;
  • Rec — ⚑ Mark that puts a mark in the take, to find the moment later; your marks and the last takes.

One phone can be the conductor — it loads, starts and stops the songs for everyone. It is set on the golem, in Settings ▸ Connect ▸ Devices (🎼). The other phones read.

An input with nobody set on it shows on no phone: the golem says so on the Devices page.
